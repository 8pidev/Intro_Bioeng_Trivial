# Draft classroom rules

1. Six groups play in a fixed turn order.
2. The active group rolls a digital six-sided die.
3. The teacher chooses the group's valid destination at junctions.
4. The group answers a question matching the destination color.
5. A correct answer on a category headquarters awards that category wedge.
6. The first group to collect all six wedges and complete the final challenge wins.

These rules are provisional and should remain configurable as the classroom
workflow is tested.
