# Classroom rules

1. Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Epsilon, and Zeta take turns in that order.
2. Roll the die and select a highlighted destination at the exact rolled distance.
3. Answer the destination category; hub landings require choosing a category first.
4. A selected answer locks. Continue scores it automatically after the explanation.
   Questions default to 30 seconds; the teacher can change the duration. At zero,
   the answer is revealed but the team earns no credit.
5. In Classroom mode a correct answer on any colored space awards its missing wedge.
6. Each answered question passes play to the next team. Roll-again spaces allow another roll.
7. With all six wedges, the next move unlocks a final question in a chosen category.
8. A correct final completes that team's game. Show results immediately or let
   the teams later in turn order finish the round. Same-round finishers share
   first place. A wrong final allows another attempt on a later turn.
9. The pausable clock suggests a 60-minute session. End class & rank finishes after
   the current question, ranking by wedges then correct answers. Exact ties share a place.
10. Classic mode uses headquarters-only wedges, keeps the turn after correct answers,
    and requires returning to the hub for the final challenge.

The teacher can override positions and wedges. Retry same question lets the
team answer the displayed question again. New unused question draws from the
same category; Change question lets the teacher choose a category.
Revealing the answer without a team choice awards no credit. Class timing is
a facilitation target, not a guarantee of a natural six-wedge victory.
